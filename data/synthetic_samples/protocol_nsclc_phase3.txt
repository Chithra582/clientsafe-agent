SECTION 4.1: INCLUSION CRITERIA
================================================================================
Patients must meet all of the following inclusion criteria to be eligible for enrollment:

[Page 4]
INC-1: Age >= 18 years at the time of signing informed consent.
INC-2: Histologically or cytologically confirmed diagnosis of Stage IV Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC) (ICD-10 C34.90).
INC-3: ECOG Performance Status score of 0 or 1 documented within 14 days prior to randomization.
INC-4: Adequate renal function defined as estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate (eGFR) >= 50 mL/min/1.73m2 by CKD-EPI formula (LOINC 33914-3).
INC-5: Adequate hematologic reserve evidenced by:
  - Absolute Neutrophil Count (ANC) >= 1500 cells/uL (LOINC 26499-4)
  - Platelet count >= 100,000 /uL (LOINC 777-3)
  - Hemoglobin >= 9.0 g/dL (LOINC 718-7)
INC-6: Adequate hepatic function:
  - Total Bilirubin <= 1.5 x Upper Limit of Normal (ULN) (LOINC 1975-2)
  - Alanine aminotransferase (ALT) and Aspartate aminotransferase (AST) <= 2.5 x ULN (LOINC 1742-6, LOINC 1920-8)
INC-7: Washout period of at least 28 days (>= 28 days) since completion of prior systemic antineoplastic chemotherapy or targeted therapy prior to Day 1 of study treatment.

================================================================================
SECTION 4.2: EXCLUSION CRITERIA
================================================================================
Patients meeting any of the following criteria must be excluded from study participation:

[Page 5]
EXC-1: Active, untreated, or symptomatic Central Nervous System (CNS) or brain metastases (SNOMED 723188008). Patients with treated, asymptomatic brain metastases are eligible if stable >= 28 days.
EXC-2: Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic immunosuppressive therapy (e.g., Crohn's disease, ulcerative colitis, rheumatoid arthritis) within the past 2 years (SNOMED 424144002, ICD-10 M05.9, K50.90).
EXC-3: Unstable cardiac disease, including New York Heart Association (NYHA) Class III or IV congestive heart failure (ICD-10 I50.9) or acute myocardial infarction within 6 months prior to screening.
EXC-4: Known active human immunodeficiency virus (HIV), active Hepatitis B, or active Hepatitis C infection with detectable viral load.
EXC-5: History of non-infectious pneumonitis or interstitial lung disease requiring systemic steroids.
EXC-6: Glycemic decompensation: Glycated Hemoglobin (HbA1c) >= 8.5% (LOINC 4548-4).
